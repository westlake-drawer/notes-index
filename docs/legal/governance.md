**性质。** Westlake Drawer 是非正式的学生协作团体，不是独立法人，不代表西湖大学，
不对外签署有约束力的合同，也不以本组织名义募集资金。一切参与均为个人身份。

**角色。**

| 角色 | 产生方式 | 权限 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 发起人 | 创建组织 | 与其他 owner 相同的组织管理权 |
| owner（维护者） | 由现有 owner 授予 | 组织设置、成员管理、仓库增删 |
| 成员 | 申请通过后受邀 | 按仓库权限提交与评审 |
| 贡献者 | 提交被合并即视为 | 无组织权限，仅贡献内容 |

**决策。** 日常事务由维护者共识决定。以下事项需多数维护者同意，并在
[community](https://github.com/westlake-drawer/community) 公示至少 7 天：

- 删除或归档仓库、迁移组织；
- 变更内容许可；
- 与外部机构合作、接受赞助；
- 修改本章程与免责相关文件。

**加入与退出。** 加入见 [membership-application](https://github.com/westlake-drawer/membership-application)；
任何成员可随时退出，无需理由。退出不影响已按 [贡献者许可](/legal/cla) 授权的内容。

**资产。** 仓库、域名与组织账号属于项目资产，不属于任何个人。项目没有独立资金账户；
若将来接受任何资金，须先制定财务规则并公示。

**解散。** 由维护者决定解散时，提前 30 天公示；仓库转为只读归档；组织账号与域名由
最后一位维护者负责移交或注销；已发布内容按其许可继续有效。

**创始人退出。** 剩余维护者可继续运营，但组织须始终保留至少一名 owner；不足时应在
社区公示并招募接手者，或按解散程序处理。

**争议。** 先按 [用户使用条款](/legal/terms) 的争议解决条款协商；内部争议由维护者多数裁决，
并保留书面记录。

**Nature.** Westlake Drawer is an informal student collaboration. It is not a legal
entity, does not represent Westlake University, does not sign binding contracts, and
does not raise funds in the project's name. All participation is personal.

**Roles.** Founders, owners (maintainers), members, and contributors — permissions
follow GitHub organization roles. Contributing a merged change confers no
organizational rights.

**Decisions.** Day-to-day matters by maintainer consensus. Deleting or archiving
repositories, changing content licences, external partnerships or sponsorship, and
changes to this document require a majority of maintainers and a 7-day notice in
[community](https://github.com/westlake-drawer/community).

**Joining and leaving.** Join via
[membership-application](https://github.com/westlake-drawer/membership-application).
Anyone may leave at any time; leaving does not revoke content already licensed
under the [contributor licence](/legal/cla).

**Assets.** Repositories, domains, and the organization account belong to the
project, not to any individual. There is no separate fund; any future money
requires published financial rules first.

**Wind-down.** 30 days' notice, repositories archived read-only, account and domain
handed over or closed by the last maintainer, published content remains valid under
its licence.

**Founder departure.** Remaining maintainers continue, provided at least one owner
remains; otherwise the project recruits a successor or winds down.

**Disputes.** Negotiation first under the [terms of use](/legal/terms); internal
disputes by majority of maintainers, with a written record.
