Westlake Drawer 是由学生以个人身份维护的课程笔记与工具索引，**不是西湖大学的官方组织**，
也不是依法登记的社会团体或法人。下列文件共同构成项目的规则体系。

Westlake Drawer is a course-note and tools index maintained by students in a personal
capacity. It is **not an official organization of Westlake University** and not a
registered association or legal entity. The documents below form the project's rules.

<LegalIndex />

各仓库的 `LICENSE`、`SECURITY.md` 与 `NOTICE` 分别适用于其规定的事项：`LICENSE` 规定授权条件，
`SECURITY.md` 规定漏洞报告与处理流程，`NOTICE` 载明第三方版权与许可信息。它们与本站文件在同一
事项上冲突时，按各自适用范围与法律效力分别解释，且不构成对任何法定义务的排除。若与强制性法律
规定冲突，以法律为准。

A repository's `LICENSE`, `SECURITY.md`, and `NOTICE` each govern what they cover. Where they
conflict with a document here on the same subject, each is interpreted by its own scope and
legal effect; none of them excludes a legal obligation. Mandatory law overrides all of them.

These texts are drafted by volunteers from public sources and are **not legal advice**.
