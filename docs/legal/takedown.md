本流程参考《信息网络传播权保护条例》的通知—删除规则，适用于本站页面与本组织仓库中的内容。

**一、提交通知。** 请提供：

1. 权利人的姓名或名称、联系方式和身份证明；
2. 权属证明（作品登记、发表记录、原始文件等）；
3. 涉嫌侵权内容的准确 URL 或仓库路径；
4. 侵权说明（哪一部分、为什么侵权）；
5. 真实性声明：你保证通知内容真实，并愿意承担因虚假通知造成的责任；
6. 善意声明：你已善意认为该使用未经授权。

提交渠道：在 [community](https://github.com/westlake-drawer/community) 提交 issue 说明情况
（**不要**在公开 issue 中写身份证号、电话等敏感信息），或通过组织页面联系维护者。

**二、我们的处理。** 收到材料完整的通知后，我们会在 **7 个工作日**内初步处理：
移除或限制访问相关内容，并记录处理时间与决定。材料不全的，我们会说明需要补充什么。

**三、反通知。** 被投诉方可提交反通知，内容包括：身份与联系方式、被移除内容的地址、
不构成侵权的理由与证据、同意接受有管辖权法院的管辖、真实性声明。
我们会在收到合格反通知后 **10 个工作日**内恢复内容，除非权利人已提起诉讼并通知我们。

**四、恶意通知。** 明显虚假或滥用通知渠道的，我们可拒绝处理并限制其后续提交。

**五、记录。** 通知、反通知与处理决定保存 24 个月，仅维护者可访问。

**六、说明。** 本流程不构成法律意见；涉及重大权利争议时，请咨询律师。

<TabItem value="en" label="English">

Modelled on the notice-and-takedown rule of China's Regulations on the Protection of
the Right to Network Dissemination of Information. It applies to site pages and to
content in this organization's repositories.

**1. Sending a notice.** Provide: the rights holder's name, contact details and
identification; proof of ownership (registration, publication record, original
files); the exact URL or repository path; what infringes and why; a statement that
the notice is accurate and that you accept responsibility for a false notice; and a
good-faith statement that the use is unauthorised.

Send it as an issue in [community](https://github.com/westlake-drawer/community)
(**never** include ID numbers, phone numbers, or other sensitive data in a public
issue), or contact a maintainer through the organization page.

**2. What we do.** Within **7 working days** of a complete notice we remove or
restrict the content and record the time and decision. If material is missing, we say
what is needed.

**3. Counter-notice.** The uploader may respond with their identity and contact
details, the location of the removed content, reasons and evidence that it is
non-infringing, consent to the jurisdiction of a competent court, and a statement of
accuracy. We restore the content within **10 working days** of a valid counter-notice
unless the rights holder has sued and told us.

**4. Abuse.** Clearly false or abusive notices may be refused, and the sender's
future submissions restricted.

**5. Records.** Notices, counter-notices, and decisions are kept for 24 months,
accessible to maintainers only.

**6. Note.** This process is not legal advice; for a serious dispute, consult a lawyer.
