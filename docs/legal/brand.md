# 名称与非官方关联 · Name and affiliation

## 中文

**非官方。** Westlake Drawer 由学生以个人身份运营，不是西湖大学的部门、学生会、附属机构
或官方项目。本站的任何内容与活动均不代表学校立场。

**名称使用。** "Westlake Drawer"是项目自用名称。"Westlake University / 西湖大学"仅用于
事实性描述（例如"面向西湖大学学生的课程笔记索引"），不用于暗示授权、认证或合作。
项目不使用校徽、校名标准字或视觉识别系统。

**成员身份。** 成员以个人身份参与。使用学校邮箱申请或贡献，不构成学校对项目的认可，
也不使学校对项目内容承担任何责任。

**地图与图片。** 校园图使用 terraink.app 导出、基于 OpenStreetMap 数据，按 ODbL 署名
`© OpenStreetMap contributors`。照片如涉及个人肖像，须取得同意。

**对外材料。** 招募、宣传与赞助材料须与本页一致：说明项目由学生自发组织、
与学校无隶属关系。

## English

**Not official.** Westlake Drawer is run by students in a personal capacity. It is not
a department, student union, affiliate, or official project of Westlake University.
Nothing here represents the university's position.

**Use of names.** "Westlake Drawer" is the project's own name. "Westlake University"
appears only in factual description (for example, "a course-note index for Westlake
University students") and never to imply authorisation, endorsement, or partnership.
The project does not use the university's seal, wordmark, or visual identity.

**Membership.** Members participate personally. Applying or contributing with a
university email grants no university endorsement and creates no university
responsibility for the content.

**Maps and images.** Campus maps are terraink.app exports based on OpenStreetMap data,
attributed `© OpenStreetMap contributors` per ODbL. Photographs of identifiable people
require consent.

**Public materials.** Recruitment, promotion, and sponsorship material must match this
page: student-run, not affiliated with the university.
