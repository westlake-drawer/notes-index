# 学术诚信 · Academic Integrity

## 中文

**笔记是学习辅助材料。** 它们帮助你理解课程，不是提交作业的素材。能否在课程作业中使用，
由课程与学校规定，不由本站决定。

**不得做。** 将他人笔记冒充为自己的原创作品；上传或传播考试答案、未公开试题与保密教学资料；
上传违反课程或课题组保密要求的材料；伪造引用、实验数据或学术成果。

**往年试题。** 只有在课程或教师允许公开时才可上传；不确定时先询问教师。

**AI 生成内容。** 若笔记部分由 AI 生成或整理，应在仓库中标注工具与范围。
AI 生成不改变版权与准确性责任：内容仍须由提交者核实，且不得包含侵权材料。

**科研资料。** 未发表成果、课题组内部文档、实验记录与受限项目材料不得上传；
涉密与受出口管制的数据一律不得公开。

**未成年人。** 若内容涉及未满 14 周岁的学生信息，不得上传；其他未成年人信息须取得监护人同意。

**违规处理。** 视情节采取：移除内容、要求更正、限制贡献权限、移出组织。
严重或重复违规的，维护者可公开说明处理结果（不披露个人信息）。

## English

**Notes are study aids**, not material to submit as your own work. Whether you may use
them in coursework is decided by your course and university, not by this site.

**Prohibited.** Passing off others' notes as your own; uploading or sharing exam
answers, unreleased exam papers, or confidential teaching material; uploading material
that breaches a course's or group's confidentiality; fabricating citations, data, or
results.

**Past exams.** Upload only where the course or instructor permits publication; ask
first if unsure.

**AI-generated content.** If part of a note was produced or organised by an AI tool,
label the tool and the scope in the repository. AI generation changes neither
copyright nor responsibility for accuracy: the submitter must verify it and must not
include infringing material.

**Research material.** Unpublished results, group-internal documents, lab records, and
restricted project material must not be uploaded; classified and export-controlled data
must never be published.

**Minors.** Do not upload information about students under 14; for other minors,
obtain guardian consent.

**Enforcement.** Depending on severity: content removal, a correction request,
restricted contribution rights, or removal from the organization. For serious or
repeated violations, maintainers may publish the outcome without personal data.
