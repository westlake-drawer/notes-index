# 第三方工具收录政策 · Tool Listing Policy

## 中文

**收录标准。** 工具须满足：源码公开可读；许可明确；有基本的维护记录（提交或发布历史）；
用途与学习或科研相关；不存在已知的恶意行为。

**标注要求。** 每个条目注明：开发者、官方仓库地址、许可、版本与更新时间。
被收录不等于经过安全审计，页面上会明确这一点。

**我们不做的事。** 本组织不开发、不打包、不分发这些工具（自研工具另行声明），
不提供安装支持，也不对其行为负责。

**下架条件。** 出现以下情况时移除或隐藏条目：包含恶意代码或钓鱼行为；侵犯第三方权利；
未经说明收集或上传用户数据；要求与其功能不符的权限；长期无人维护且存在已知漏洞；
权利人提出有效通知；作者要求下架。

**安全报告。** 发现工具存在漏洞或恶意行为，请按[侵权或安全流程](/legal/security)私下报告，
不要公开复现细节。

**更新。** 工具发布新版本后，若已知问题未修复或权限范围扩大，我们会重新评估其条目。

**未来变化。** 若本组织将来提供安装包、在线执行环境或托管服务，须在提供前另行完成审查，
并更新本页。

## English

**Listing criteria.** Publicly readable source; a clear licence; some maintenance
history; a study or research purpose; no known malicious behaviour.

**Labelling.** Each entry names the developer, the official repository, the licence,
the version, and the last update. Being listed is not a security audit, and pages say so.

**What we do not do.** The organization does not develop, package, or distribute these
tools (in-house tools are stated separately), does not provide installation support,
and is not responsible for their behaviour.

**Removal.** Entries are removed or hidden when a tool contains malware or phishing,
infringes third-party rights, collects or uploads user data without disclosure, asks
for permissions beyond its function, is unmaintained with known vulnerabilities, or on
a valid rights notice or the author's request.

**Reporting.** Report vulnerabilities or malicious behaviour privately through the
[security process](/legal/security), not with public reproduction steps.

**Updates.** New releases that leave known issues unfixed or widen permissions trigger
a fresh review of the entry.

**Future changes.** If the organization ever ships installers, an execution
environment, or hosted services, a separate review must be completed first and this
page updated.
