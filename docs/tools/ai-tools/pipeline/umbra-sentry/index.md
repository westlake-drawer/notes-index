# Umbra Sentry

<div className="repo-meta"><span>Pipeline</span><span>Python</span><span>v1.0.0</span><span>Security</span></div>

[**umbra-sentry**](https://github.com/JiamingShi-JsPhys/umbra-sentry) hardens your own release artifacts against AI reverse-engineering agents. A poisoned analyst burns tokens on canaries, drowns in budget breakers, and reconstructs shadow decoys — while every action it takes is typed, gated, and exportable to OCSF and Sigma.

Defense here is not obfuscation: it is semantic deception, deterministic chaining, and decoupled response. Five strategies and two adapters are included.
