# 权利、许可与学术诚信 / Rights, licensing, and academic integrity

> 本页补充 `CONTRIBUTING.md`，提交前请一并阅读。
> This page supplements `CONTRIBUTING.md`; read both before submitting.

## 中文

**你只能提交你有权提交的内容。** 提交即表示你同意
[贡献者许可协议](https://westlake-drawer.github.io/legal/cla)：
你保留著作权，并授予本组织展示、索引、缓存、格式转换所需的非独占许可。

- 不得上传教师课件原文、习题答案、教材扫描件、课程视频或录音。
- 不得上传未发表科研数据、课题组内部资料或受限项目材料。
- 复制他人材料前先确认许可，并保留署名。
- 注明来源不等于获得授权。

**学术诚信。** 笔记是学习辅助材料。不得用于冒充原创作业；往年试题需课程允许才可上传；
AI 生成或整理的内容须在仓库中标注工具与范围。
详见 [学术诚信](https://westlake-drawer.github.io/legal/academic-integrity)。

**侵权处理。** 若你认为某内容侵权，可通过
[通知流程](https://westlake-drawer.github.io/legal/takedown) 提出，我们会在 7 个工作日内初步处理。

## English

**Submit only what you have the right to submit.** Submission accepts the
[contributor licence](https://westlake-drawer.github.io/legal/cla): you keep the
copyright and grant the organization the non-exclusive licence needed to display,
index, cache, and reformat the content.

- Do not upload instructor slides or handouts, problem-set solutions, textbook
  scans, lecture recordings, or protected course-site material.
- Do not upload unpublished research, group-internal documents, or restricted
  project material.
- Clear permission before copying third-party material, and keep attribution.
- Citing a source is not a licence.

**Academic integrity.** Notes are study aids. Do not use them as your own submitted
work; past exams only where the course allows it; label AI-generated or AI-organised
content with the tool and scope. See
[academic integrity](https://westlake-drawer.github.io/legal/academic-integrity).

**Takedown.** Report infringement through the
[notice process](https://westlake-drawer.github.io/legal/takedown); we act within 7
working days.
